People remember what made them laugh. The leaked neuroscience research shows that humor triggers dopamine release, making content more memorable and shareable. Yet many creators avoid humor, fearing it's unprofessional or risky. Strategic humor, done well, builds connection like nothing else.

Why Humor Goals Matter

In a sea of serious content, humor stands out. The leaked engagement data shows that funny posts receive 2-3x more shares than serious ones. People share what makes them laugh because they want others to laugh too.

Humor also humanizes you. It shows you don't take yourself too seriously, making you more relatable and approachable. Audiences connect with humans, not brands.

Humor Style Goals

Different humor styles suit different audiences and personalities.

Humor Types Goals

Choose humor styles that fit your brand.

  • Self-deprecating - Laugh at yourself (builds humility)
  • Observational - Funny truths about daily life (relatable)
  • Industry insider - Inside jokes about your field (builds community)
  • Wordplay/puns - Clever language (shows wit)
  • Absurdist - Unexpected, silly content (memorable)
  • Satire - Gentle mocking of trends (shows insight)

Humor Intensity Goals

Vary how funny your content is.

Intensity Description Frequency
Mild humor Light wit, gentle fun Daily
Medium humor Clear funny, intended laugh Weekly
High humor Very funny, shareable Monthly
Risk humor Edgy, might not land Occasional

Humor Topic Goals

What subjects will you find humor in?

Safe Humor Topics Goals

Topics that are generally safe for humor.

  • Industry quirks - Funny things about your field
  • Universal struggles - Things everyone experiences
  • Everyday situations - Relatable daily moments
  • Technology fails - Tech problems everyone knows
  • Your own mistakes - Self-deprecating humor
  • Pop culture references - Shared cultural touchpoints

Risky Humor Topics Goals

Topics that require careful handling.

Risky Humor Guidelines

Politics - Avoid unless core to brand
Religion - Generally avoid
Sensitive current events - Avoid
Marginalized groups - Never punch down
Tragedies - Never appropriate
Controversial figures - Know your audience

Humor Testing Goals

Test humor to see what lands with your audience.

Humor Feedback Goals

Gather data on what's funny to your audience.

Feedback Method What to Track Goal
Engagement rates Likes, comments, shares 2x average

Humor Consistency Goals

Incorporate humor regularly, not just occasionally.

  • Daily humor - At least one funny element daily (witty caption, funny observation)
  • Weekly funny post - One post primarily for humor weekly
  • Monthly comedy content - One significant humorous piece monthly
  • Humor in stories - Daily funny moments in stories
  • Seasonal humor - Holiday and event-related humor

Humor Boundaries Goals

Know where your humor lines are.

  • Never punch down - Humor at expense of marginalized groups is off-limits
  • Know your audience - Humor that works for one group may offend another
  • Test before committing - Try new humor styles on small scale first
  • Have an apology protocol - Plan for when humor misses
  • Stay true to yourself - Don't force humor that doesn't fit your personality

Content humor makes you memorable and relatable. By setting strategic goals for style, topics, and testing, you can make your audience laugh while building genuine connection. Start implementing these humor goals today and watch your content become more shareable.
