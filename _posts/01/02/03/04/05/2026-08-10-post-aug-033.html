The best content ideas often come directly from your audience. Their questions reveal exactly what they want to know. The leaked content planning data shows that QandA content consistently outperforms creator-chosen topics because it addresses real needs.

Why QandA Goals Matter

When you answer someone's question directly, you provide immediate value. The person who asked feels seen and heard. Others with the same question get answered too. The leaked satisfaction data shows that QandA content generates the highest gratitude responses from audiences.

QandA content also ensures you're creating what people actually want. Many creators guess at topics; QandA removes the guesswork.

Question Collection Goals

Gather questions systematically from your audience.

Question Sources Goals

Where will you collect questions from?

  • Comments section - Monitor comments for questions (daily)
  • Direct messages - Save DMs with questions (weekly review)
  • QandA prompts - Explicitly ask for questions (weekly)
  • Polls and surveys - Ask what they want to know (monthly)
  • Email submissions - Allow questions via email
  • Live sessions - Real-time questions during lives

Question Volume Goals

How many questions will you collect?

Monthly 100+ questions 20-30 comprehensive answers
Quarterly 300+ questions QandA compilation

Question Prioritization Goals

Not all questions deserve equal attention. Prioritize strategically.

Question Value Goals

Choose questions that provide most value.

  • Frequency asked - Questions that come up repeatedly
  • Broad relevance - Questions many people have
  • Depth potential - Questions that allow comprehensive answers
  • Urgency - Time-sensitive questions
  • Expertise alignment - Questions in your sweet spot
  • Uniqueness - Questions others aren't answering

QandA Format Goals

Different formats for different types of questions.

QandA Format Mix Goals

Use various formats to answer questions.

QandA Format Mix

Quick answer posts - Daily (short answers)
Dedicated QandA posts - Weekly (3-5 questions)
QandA videos - Monthly (video answers)
Live QandA sessions - Monthly (real-time)
QandA compilations - Quarterly (best of)
FAQ pages - Annual (comprehensive)

Answer Quality Goals

Ensure answers provide real value.

Quality Dimension Goal
Clarity Easy to understand
Completeness Fully answers the question
Actionability Can apply the answer
Evidence Support with examples/data
Respect No question is stupid

QandA Engagement Goals

QandA content naturally drives more questions.

  • Follow-up questions - Encourage deeper inquiry
  • Question threads - Create ongoing QandA conversations
  • Question of the day/week - Regular feature
  • Answered questions archive - Searchable resource
  • Community answers - Let audience answer each other

QandA Measurement Goals

Track the impact of your QandA content.

  • Question volume trend - Are more people asking?
  • Answer engagement - How do QandA posts perform?
  • Gratitude responses - Thank you comments and DMs
  • Question repetition - Are you eliminating recurring questions?
  • Search traffic - Do people find answers via search?
  • FAQ coverage - Percentage of common questions answered
