People consume content primarily to solve problems. Whether it's a practical issue, an emotional need, or a knowledge gap, they're looking for solutions. The leaked user intent data shows that problem-solving content has the highest value to audiences and the highest conversion rates.

Why Problem-Solving Goals Matter

When you solve someone's problem, you become valuable to them. They remember you when similar issues arise. The leaked loyalty data shows that problem-solving content creates the strongest long-term relationships because it provides tangible value.

Problem-solving also positions you as an authority. People trust those who have helped them before.

Problem Identification Goals

Find the problems your audience needs solved.

Problem Discovery Goals

Where will you find problems to solve?

  • Audience questions - Problems they're asking about (daily)
  • Comments and complaints - Frustrations they express (daily)
  • Industry gaps - Problems no one is solving (monthly)
  • Your own experience - Problems you've faced (weekly)
  • Competitor gaps - What competitors miss (monthly)
  • Trend analysis - Emerging problems (quarterly)

Which problems to solve first?

Solution Quality Goals

Ensure your solutions actually work.

Solution Effectiveness Goals

How will you measure if your solutions work?

  • Solution testing - Try solutions yourself first
  • Audience feedback - Ask if solutions worked
  • Success stories - Collect examples of solutions working
  • Iteration - Improve solutions based on feedback
  • Alternative solutions - Offer multiple approaches

Problem-Solving Impact Goals

Measure how well you're solving problems.

  • Problem resolution rate - How many problems solved?
  • Solution satisfaction - Audience feedback on solutions
  • Repeat problem reduction - Are problems staying solved?
  • Problem mentions - Do people mention your solutions?
  • Gratitude metrics - Thank you messages
  • Solution sharing - People sharing solutions with others

Problem-solving content makes you indispensable to your audience.
