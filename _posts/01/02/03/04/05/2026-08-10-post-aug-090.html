Creating all content yourself limits your scale. The most efficient creators enlist their audience as content creators. The leaked UGC strategies from community-driven brands show how to set goals that encourage, capture, and leverage user-generated content. When your audience creates for you, everyone wins.

Why UGC Goals Multiply Your Efforts

User-generated content provides social proof that your own content cannot. When potential customers see real people using and loving your offerings, trust increases. The leaked conversion data from brands using UGC shows 2-3x higher conversion rates compared to brand-created content alone.

UGC also scales your content production. Each piece of user content adds to your library without costing your time. A thriving UGC program can generate more content than you could create alone.

Types of UGC Goals

Set goals across the UGC lifecycle.

Collection Goals

How much UGC will you gather?

  • Monthly submissions - Target number of UGC pieces received
  • Participation rate - Percentage of audience contributing
  • Campaign responses - UGC from specific prompts or contests
  • Hashtag usage - Posts using your branded hashtag
  • Mentions - Times you're tagged in relevant content

Quality Goals

Not all UGC deserves promotion. Set quality standards.

Quality Dimension Minimum Standard Ideal Standard
Visual quality Clear, not blurry Well-composed, good lighting
Message alignment Positive sentiment Specific value mentioned
Authenticity Real person, real experience Story or transformation shared

Encouraging UGC Creation

People need motivation to create content for you. Set goals for UGC prompts.

Prompt Frequency Goals

How often will you ask for UGC?


UGC Prompt Schedule:
- Weekly: Simple question or photo request in stories
- Monthly: Themed UGC campaign with specific prompt
- Quarterly: Contest or challenge with incentives
- Annually: Major campaign with significant rewards

Incentive Structure:
- Featured on your page (recognition)
- Small prizes or discounts
- Grand prizes for top submissions
- Community recognition and badges

Campaign Goals

For each UGC campaign, set specific targets.

UGC Curation and Selection Goals

Not all submissions get featured. Set goals for curation.

Selection Rate Goals

What percentage of submissions will you feature?

  • Daily feature goal - 1-3 pieces of UGC featured daily
  • Selection rate - Feature 10-20% of quality submissions
  • Response to non-featured - Thank all submitters, explain selection process
  • Diversity goals - Represent different audience segments

Feature Quality Goals

When featuring UGC, add value.

Feature Enhancement

Don't just repost. Add context, highlight specific elements, tag the creator, explain why you're featuring them, and encourage others to participate.

UGC Performance Goals

Track how UGC performs compared to your content.

Metric UGC Performance Goal
Engagement rate Typically higher than brand content 20% above average
Trust signals Higher conversion when featured Track attribution
Community growth Featured creators often become advocates 10% become regular contributors

Legal and Ethical UGC Goals

Using others' content requires responsibility.

  • Permission rate - Always get explicit permission before reposting
  • Attribution rate - Always credit creator clearly
  • Response rate - Reply to all UGC creators
  • Removal process - Easy opt-out if creators request removal
  • Terms clarity - Clear guidelines for UGC submissions

User-generated content multiplies your content while building community. By setting strategic goals for collection, quality, and curation, you leverage your audience's creativity. Start implementing these UGC goals today and watch your content library expand through community contribution.
