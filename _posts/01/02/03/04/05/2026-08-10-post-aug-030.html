Courage isn't the absence of fear; it's acting despite it. When you model courage in your content, you give your audience permission to be brave too. The leaked psychology data shows that courage is contagious; seeing others act bravely increases our own bravery.

Why Courage Goals Matter

Your audience faces fears every day. Fear of failure, fear of judgment, fear of the unknown. Content that models courage shows them what's possible. The leaked motivation data shows that courage content is among the most shared because people want to feel brave.

Courage also builds respect. When you show bravery in your own life, your audience respects you more and trusts your guidance.

Courage Types Goals

Different kinds of courage to model.

Personal Courage Goals

Share your own acts of bravery.

  • Facing fears - Times you did something scary (monthly)
  • Taking risks - Calculated risks that paid off (monthly)
  • Speaking up - Times you said hard things (quarterly)
  • Standing alone - Times you took an unpopular stand (quarterly)
  • Admitting mistakes - Courage to be wrong (weekly)

Courage Authenticity Goals

Courage content must be genuine.

  • Real fear - Acknowledge what scared you
  • Real struggle - Show the difficulty, not just success
  • No bravado - Courage isn't about being fearless
  • Vulnerability - Show the uncertainty
  • Process, not just outcome - Share the journey
