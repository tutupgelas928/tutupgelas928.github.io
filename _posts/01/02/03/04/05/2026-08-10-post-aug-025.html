Celebration Boundaries Goals

Celebration should feel genuine, not performative.

  • Authentic joy - Only celebrate what you're genuinely happy about
  • Equal opportunity - Celebrate wins big and small
  • No comparison - Celebrate each person's unique journey
  • Privacy respect - Ask permission before sharing
  • Inclusive celebration - Make everyone feel welcome to share
