Leaked Single-Post Emotional Map Template

This is the foundational worksheet used by agency content strategists to plan every individual Reel, TikTok, or post. Copy this structure into your preferred note-taking app.

EMOTIONAL JOURNEY MAP - SINGLE POST
====================================
POST IDEA/TITLE: [What is the core topic?]

TARGET AUDIENCE EMOTION BEFORE SCROLL:
[ ] Bored        [ ] Stressed       [ ] Curious       [ ] Overwhelmed
[ ] Happy        [ ] Frustrated     [ ] Anxious       [ ] Other: ____

--- STAGE 1: THE HOOK (0-3 seconds) ---
EMOTION TO TRIGGER: [ ] Curiosity [ ] Recognition [ ] Surprise [ ] Intrigue
VISUAL HOOK: [Describe the first frame]
TEXT/COPY HOOK: [First words or text overlay]
AUDIO HOOK: [Sound, music, or first spoken words]

--- STAGE 2: THE BUILD (4-15s or equivalent) ---
EMOTION TO ESCALATE: [ ] Tension [ ] Desire [ ] Confusion (to be solved)
KEY MESSAGE/METHOD: [How will you agitate or deepen the hook?]
VISUAL/STRUCTURE: [Quick cuts? Story? Data?]

--- STAGE 3: THE PAYOFF (Main Value) ---
EMOTION TO DELIVER: [ ] Relief [ ] Empowerment [ ] Clarity [ ] Joy
CORE VALUE: [The tip, tutorial, reveal, or insight]
FORMAT: [Step-by-step, demonstration, before/after, list]
VISUAL FOCUS: [What will make this satisfying to watch/read?]

--- STAGE 4: THE BRIDGE (Call-to-Action) ---
EMOTION TO CHANNEL: [ ] Belonging [ ] Anticipation [ ] Satisfaction
PRIMARY CTA: [Comment prompt? Follow? Save? Link click?]
EXACT PHRASING: [Write the final line or screen text]
SECONDARY GOAL: [What should they feel/think after engaging?]

--- METRICS TO TRACK (Post-Publishing) ---
[ ] Completion Rate (Plays at 100% or scroll depth)
[ ] Save/Pin/Bookmark Count
[ ] Emotional Sentiment of Comments (Positive/Story-based)
[ ] Shares (Especially with context)

How to Use This Template: Fill this out for your next 3 pieces of content BEFORE you create anything. Force yourself to define the emotion for each stage. This pre-production step, highlighted in the leak, is what separates amateurs from pros. It turns anxious creation into confident execution.

Pro Tip from the Leak: Keep a library of your completed templates. After 10 posts, analyze which emotional combinations performed best. Build your personal "Emotional Blueprint" based on what works for YOUR audience.

Notion & Airtable Database Setups From The Leak

The leak included screenshots of the actual Notion and Airtable databases used to manage this strategy at scale. Here are the key configurations you can replicate.

Notion Database Setup

Create a database with the following properties (Columns):

  • Title: Content Idea
  • Select: Status (Idea, Planned, Scripting, Filming, Editing, Scheduled, Published, Analyzing)
  • Select: Platform (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc.)
  • Select: Content Format (Reel, Carousel, Story, Long Video, etc.)
  • Select: Primary Emotion Goal (Curiosity, Relief, Empowerment, etc.)
  • Multi-Select: Journey Stage Tags (Hook, Build, Payoff, Bridge)
  • Text: Hook Copy (Write the exact hook)
  • Text: Payoff Value (The core lesson/tip)
  • Text: Bridge CTA (Exact call-to-action)
  • URL: Published Link
  • Number: ECR Score (Emotional Completion Rate % - filled post-publish)
  • Number: Save Rate
  • Relation: Link to a "Series" database if part of a campaign.
Create views filtered by Status, Platform, and Primary Emotion Goal to see your content pipeline through different lenses.

Airtable Base Setup

Airtable allows for more complex relations. The leaked setup had three linked tables:

  1. Content Pieces Table: Similar fields to Notion, plus a linked record to "Emotional Archetypes."
  2. Emotional Archetypes Table: A library of pre-defined emotional journeys (e.g., "The Reliever": Problem Hook → Agitate Build → Solution Payoff → Community Bridge). Each content piece selects an archetype as a starting template.
  3. Performance Metrics Table: A separate table where each published piece gets a new record weekly, tracking ECR, ERR, ESV over time. Linked to the Content Piece. This allows for trend analysis.
The power of Airtable is visualizing the data: create a "Emotional Performance Dashboard" view with charts showing which Emotional Archetypes yield the highest ECR and ESV for each platform.

Whether you choose the simplicity of Notion or the power of Airtable, the key is having a centralized system where the emotional strategy is a defined field, not just a note in a doc. This operationalizes the framework.

4. Emotional Journey Mapping Cheat Sheet

A quick-reference guide from the leaked documents:

HOOK EMOTIONS (0-3s):
- Curiosity: "What if...?" "Why does...?"
- Recognition: "You feel..." "If you've ever..."
- Surprise: "The secret..." "Nobody tells you..."
- Intrigue: "What happens when..." "I discovered..."

BUILD EMOTIONS (4-15s):
- Tension: Show consequences, raise stakes
- Desire: Make outcome incredibly appealing
- Confusion: Create "need to know"
- Empathy: "I've been there too"

PAYOFF EMOTIONS (Main Value):
- Relief: Problem solved
- Empowerment: "I can do this!"
- Clarity: "Now I understand"
- Joy: Pure enjoyment/entertainment

BRIDGE EMOTIONS (CTA):
- Belonging: Join community, share story
- Anticipation: Follow for part 2
- Satisfaction: Completed journey feeling
- Inspiration: Motivated to take action
